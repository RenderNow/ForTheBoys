Decision mentor

Safeguarding first

Not therapy. Not diagnosis. Not a replacement for trusted adults.

The mentor should support everyday reflection and emotional regulation while clearly routing serious issues to urgent human help.

  • No mental health diagnosis
  • No secrecy from trusted adults
  • No harmful or violent advice
  • Crisis and safeguarding routing
